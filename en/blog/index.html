  1. Nov 9, 2026Training Theory

    Loop-μP: Residual Scaling and Hyperparameter Transfer in Looped Transformers

    Looping is not extra depth: scale each residual branch by 1/N, and the learning rate transfers across loop counts

    Why must the learning rate be retuned when a looped Transformer runs more loops? Shared weights make the residual increments of every loop add along one direction. With each residual branch scaled by 1/N, the learning rate tuned on the smallest model stays best at 8 loops and 384 executed layers.

    SMELT: Scaling Laws for Budget-Matched Looped Transformers

    With compute, parameters, and KV cache all matched, looping still yields a net gain for MoE models

    Do Looped Transformers gain from the looped architecture itself, or from the extra compute they spend? With per-token FLOPs, total non-embedding parameters, and KV cache all matched, we find that looping beats the Baseline across the board on both loss and benchmarks.
