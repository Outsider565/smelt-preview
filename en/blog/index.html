    Puro-2B: How Can a Poor Lab Pretrain a Model from Scratch?

    Matching Qwen2-1.5B on RTX 5090 GPUs—with a $5,090 budget

    On RTX 5090 GPUs, Puro-2B matches Qwen2-1.5B on the average score across 15 tasks with about $4,400 in pretraining compute—under $5,090. We cover the hardware, FP8, optimizer, and data recipe, and provide the code, data, training configurations, and model weights needed to reproduce the experiments.
